# Drone Warfare Simulator

An original combined-arms drone flight simulator. Fly a stabilized quadcopter or a long-endurance wing across five natural battlefields while armor, infantry, air defense, and logistics move underneath.

This is not affiliated with Gaijin Entertainment or War Thunder. It is a browser simulator in that spirit: external camera, a living ground war, and airframes that do not fly like arcade spaceships.

## Airframes

| Airframe | Kind | Endurance | Munitions | How it flies |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kestrel Quad | Multirotor | About 22 minutes | 4 | Hovers. W/S translate, A/D yaw, Shift/Ctrl climb, Q/E strafe. |
| Albatross LE | Long-endurance wing | About 90 minutes | 6 | Cannot hover. W/S throttle, A/D roll into the turn, arrows pitch, Q/E rudder. Stalls if you get slow. |

Space releases a munition when a hostile is in the seeker cone. C cycles chase, nose, gimbal, and tactical cameras. V toggles battle layers. P pauses. R is an autopilot recovery.

On a phone, the left stick is move and the right stick is altitude (quad) or pitch (wing).

## Maps

- **Kharif Basin** — arid wash, hard sun, armor in the low ground
- **Blackpine Ridge** — dawn forest and fog
- **Harbor Reach** — tide flats, pier, wind off the water
- **Glacier Cut** — rock, snow, thin air for the wing
- **Vale Outskirts** — fields running into low concrete

## Battle layers

Armor, infantry, air defense, logistics, structures, and IFF markers can be switched independently. Hostile air defense shoots back. Five hostile tanks are the armor-hunt objectives. Endurance survey asks you to hold three stations long enough for a clean sensor pass.

## Android

The simulator runs in the mobile browser, with on-screen sticks sized for a phone. Add it to the home screen from the browser for an app-like icon. A native sideload APK is not in this repository: producing one needs the Android SDK and a binary upload, and neither is available for this project. The playable build is the web app.

## Scoring

Main battle tanks, IFVs, air defense, trucks, and rifle teams are worth points. Friendly forces are not valid locks. Best score is kept on this device.
